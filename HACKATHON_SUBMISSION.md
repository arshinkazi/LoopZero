# LoopZero: Environmental Hacks 2026 submission

**Summary:** A citizen platform that converts repeated observations of local environmental problems into prioritized, printable evidence.

**Problem:** One-off complaints about drains, garbage, leaks and dust are dismissed, and persistence is never documented.

**Innovation:** Deterministic hotspot grouping (locality + category + landmark), a transparent LoopZero priority score from observation span, recurrence and severity, and duplicate safeguards so same-day and repeated-text reports are flagged and not counted. The app does not claim a problem is still present: span and age are shown separately.

**Environmental impact:** Faster, better-evidenced attention to sanitation, waste, water loss and dust hazards in small towns.

**AWS usage:** Deployment target is AWS Amplify Hosting. Status: not yet deployed. The reporting workflow has no AWS dependency, so the demo works offline.

**Demo:** Open the app, select "Lane behind Sabzi Mandi", read the score breakdown and timeline, add an observation, mark it Under Review, then print the evidence report.

**Pilot goals:** One ward, 10 volunteers, 30 days. Targets: 50 independent observations, 5 persistent hotspots documented, 3 evidence reports shared with a local body.
